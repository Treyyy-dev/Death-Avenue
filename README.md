# Death Avenue

A sideways (landscape) robbery game. You're a robber in a life-size city: grab a gun at your house, hit the bank, the market or the gas station, lose the cops to keep the cash, then spend it in the stores.

Play: https://treyyy-dev.github.io/Death-Avenue/

Tap Start. The first time, you make your character; after that you go straight into the city. The game always plays sideways and full screen (on iPhone, add it to your Home Screen for full screen); if you hold the phone upright, the game turns itself sideways. On a phone, the left stick moves you (push it all the way to run) and dragging anywhere else looks around. On a computer, use WASD to move, drag to look, E to interact, F or Space to shoot and R to reload.

The city: your house (with your crew, Jordan and Maya), First City Bank, Fresh Market, the Gas & Go, Central Station with a train, two apartment buildings, the police station, and eight stores: two gun stores (Lock & Load, Avenue Arms), two barbershops (Fades, Clean Cuts), two clothing stores (Threads, Drip Co.) and two shoe stores (Kicks Corner, Sole City). Around 30 people live in it: clerks, a bank teller, barbers, patrol cops and people walking around.

Robbing: hold a gun and use the teller or a register to rob it. Crack the bank vault by staying next to it while it drills, then grab the cash bags. Stolen cash is "hot" until you get away: stay 28 meters from every cop for 20 seconds (faster if you lay low at your house). If a cop catches you, you're busted and lose the hot cash.

Inventory: guns you pick up go into the 4 slots at the bottom of the screen. Tap a slot to switch guns (tap the one you're holding to reload), and tap the bag to see 8 more slots, swap guns in and out, or drop one. On a computer, 1-4 switch slots and B opens the bag. If you're busted, the cops take your guns.

Stores: gun stores sell all seven guns, barbershops change your hair and facial hair, clothing stores sell tops, bottoms and accessories, and shoe stores sell shoes. Each costs money you've gotten away with.

Talking: tap the mic and talk to whoever you face. They answer in a speech bubble over their head. Replies come from a small AI that runs on each player's phone (WebLLM), so chatting is free and unlimited for everyone. It downloads once, then works offline. Phones that can't run it get simple built-in replies, and you can optionally add a Claude key for smarter answers. Download or delete the brain, and pick a frame rate (30, 60, 90 or 120), from the gear on the start screen.
