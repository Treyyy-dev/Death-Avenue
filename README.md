# Death Avenue

An AR robbery game. You're a robber in a life-size city: grab a gun at your house, hit the bank, the market or the gas station, and lose the cops to keep the cash.

Play: https://treyyy-dev.github.io/Death-Avenue/

Tap Start. The first time, you make your character; after that you go straight into the city. In Chrome on Android you can physically walk around; on other phones you turn the phone to look. The stick moves you further, and pushing it all the way makes you run.

The city: your house (with your crew, Jordan and Maya), First City Bank, Fresh Market, Fades Barbershop, the Gas & Go, Central Station with a train, two apartment buildings and the police station. Around 25 people live in it: clerks, a bank teller, barbers, patrol cops and people walking around.

Robbing: hold a gun and use the teller or a register to rob it. Crack the bank vault by staying next to it while it drills, then grab the cash bags. Stolen cash is "hot" until you get away: stay 28 meters from every cop for 20 seconds (faster if you lay low at your house). If a cop catches you, you're busted and lose the hot cash.

Hands: turn on Hands and hold a hand in front of the phone. Pinch your thumb and index finger to pick up a gun or use something, make a fist to shoot, and open your hand wide to drop the gun. With hands off, use the Interact and Shoot buttons. Blood can be turned off with the Blood button.

Talking: tap the mic and talk to whoever you face. They answer in a speech bubble over their head. Replies come from a small AI that runs on each player's phone (WebLLM), so chatting is free and unlimited for everyone. It downloads once, then works offline. Phones that can't run it get simple built-in replies, and you can optionally add a Claude key for smarter answers. Download or delete the brain, and pick a frame rate (30, 60, 90 or 120), from the gear on the start screen.
