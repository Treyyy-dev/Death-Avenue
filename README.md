# Room Mates

Design life-size people and drop them into your room with AR. They walk around, sit on real seats and react when you bump into them.

Play: https://treyyy-dev.github.io/Roommates/

You explore a life-size virtual room by moving your phone. In Chrome on Android you can physically walk around it; on other phones you turn the phone to look and use the stick to walk.

GPS mode puts your own character on a real satellite map that follows you as you walk. Tap your house to go inside with your people.
